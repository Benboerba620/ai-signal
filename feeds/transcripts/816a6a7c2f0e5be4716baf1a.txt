WTT: You Can't See Everything
Nov 12, 2026
The new documentary about Elizabeth Holmes and a candid
conversation with a successful investment manager got me thinking
about how we discover the truth. In this What Ted's
Thinking , You Can't See Everything, I explore how the
questions allocators ask can shape the answers they receive, and
how our search for certainty can get in the way of
understanding.

× About the Podcast
Allocator and asset management expert, Ted Seides, conducts in-depth interviews with leaders in the institutional investing industry. Guests include Chief Investment Officers from leading allocators, asset managers, strategists, thought leaders, and many more. Our mission is to learn,
, and help implement the process of premier investors. Learn more and join our community at capitalallocators.com.
